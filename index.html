Blossom Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

The short version

Nothing you log ever leaves your phone. Blossom is a cycle journal that keeps your entries only on your device. There are no accounts, no servers and no analytics. We (Tethered Systems, the developer) never see, receive or store anything you enter.

Blossom is free and shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob. The ad uses the internet, so Google receives the usual technical details of an ad request. It never receives your logs, dates, symptoms, notes, settings or chosen words. Ads are not personalised and Blossom does not use your advertising ID. A one-time Remove ads purchase turns ads off for good.

Your journal only leaves your phone if you choose to export an encrypted backup file and pick where to save it.

1Who we are

Blossom (package name com.blossomtracker.app) is made by Tethered Systems. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the Blossom Android app. You can reach us at tethered.systems@gmail.com.

2What is stored, and where

Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read it, and we cannot either. This includes:

  • Period start and end dates
  • Daily details you choose to log: flow, mood, energy, symptoms, basal body temperature and notes
  • Your reminders and app settings, including any words you chose to show in place of the standard ones
  • If you turn on app lock, a salted, one-way hash of your PIN (never the PIN itself)

Predictions (next period, ovulation and fertile window), the calendar and the Insights statistics and charts are all calculated on your phone from your own history.

Android's cloud backup is switched off for Blossom, so your journal is not copied to Google Drive or moved to a new phone automatically. If you change phones, export a backup first (see below).

3Export, import and encryption

Export is the only way your data can leave your phone, and it only happens when you start it.

  • When you export, you choose a passphrase. Blossom encrypts your data with AES-256-GCM, using a key made from your passphrase, and saves a .bls file to a place you pick.
  • Your PIN and app-lock settings are not included in the export.
  • Anyone who has both the file and your passphrase can read it. If you save the file to a cloud folder, it will be stored there under that service's terms, not ours.
  • We cannot recover a forgotten passphrase. We never have it.
  • Import replaces all data in the app with the contents of the backup. It does not merge.

4Ads and purchases

The banner ad. Blossom shows one banner at the bottom of its main screens, served by Google AdMob. To fetch an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends Google the information any ad request carries, such as your IP address (from which Google can estimate your rough location), device model and Android version, app version, and whether the ad was shown or tapped. Google uses this to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • No app data is shared. Blossom never passes anything from your journal to the ad SDK: no logs, dates, predictions, symptoms, notes, reminders, settings or the words you chose. Ad requests carry no keywords or targeting.
  • Not personalised. Blossom asks Google for non-personalised ads, and removes Android's advertising ID permission and the Privacy Sandbox ad permissions (ad ID, Topics and attribution) from the app, so ads are not tied to an ID that follows you across apps.
  • Suitable content. Ads are limited to content rated for teens and up.
  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the UK, the EEA and Switzerland), Google's consent form asks for your choice before ads load. You can change it any time in Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices.
  • Offline. Without internet, no ad is shown and every feature works exactly the same.

Remove ads. You can remove ads with a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own terms. Google Play gives us the order details it shares with developers, such as order number, product, price and country. We never receive your card details, and the purchase is never linked to anything you log. Once ads are removed, Blossom never starts the ad SDK again, so it no longer contacts the ad network. Blossom remembers the purchase on your phone. Delete all data does not remove it, and Restore purchase gets it back on a new phone signed in to the same Google account.

5What we don't do

  • No uploading of your journal. The internet is used only for the banner ad and for Google Play purchases.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising ID.
  • No third-party code besides Google's ads, consent and Play Billing libraries described above.
  • No sharing and no selling of your journal, to anyone, ever. We don't have it to share.

Google Play itself may show us general statistics, such as install counts and anonymous crash or performance reports, according to your own Android and Google settings. That comes from Google Play, not from Blossom, and never includes anything you logged in the app.

6Permissions and why we ask

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) To show the reminders you set up. You can say no; the journal still works.
  • Alarms and reminders (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) So reminders arrive at the time you chose. If you turn this off, reminders may arrive a little late.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) To set your reminders again after your phone restarts.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT) Only if you turn on app lock with fingerprint or face unlock. Android handles the check; Blossom never receives your fingerprint or face data.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE) So reminder notifications can vibrate.
  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) Only to load the banner ad, to wait for a connection before trying again, and for Google Play purchases. Your journal is never sent.
  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) For the optional Remove ads purchase.
  • Keep awake and foreground service (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE) These are added automatically by Android's WorkManager library (used by the home-screen widget library and the ads SDK). Blossom does not use them directly.

Blossom does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, accounts, storage or advertising ID access.

7Keeping things discreet

Blossom shows up on your phone with a plain notebook icon. With discreet mode on (the default), notifications only say "Blossom" and "You have a reminder", the widget hides details, and the recent-apps preview is blanked. You can also add a PIN or biometric lock.

8Children

Blossom is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect personal information ourselves, from anyone. Ads are non-personalised and limited to content rated for teens and up.

9Deleting your data

You are always in control:

  • In the app, go to Settings → Privacy → Delete all data. This erases your logs, settings and reminders from the phone.
  • Or simply uninstall Blossom. Android removes the app's private storage with it.
  • Backup files you exported are yours. Delete them wherever you saved them.

We have no copy of your journal, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For data Google holds from ad requests, see Google's page and your Google account settings.

10Health information

Blossom is not medical advice, not a medical device and not birth control. It does not diagnose anything. Predictions are estimates based on your own history and can be wrong, especially if your cycles are irregular. Do not use Blossom to prevent pregnancy or to make medical decisions. Talk to a doctor or nurse about anything that worries you.

11Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. We will never start sending your journal anywhere. Any change to what the app sends will be made in this policy first and made clear in the app's release notes.
